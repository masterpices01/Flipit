# Privacy Policy for Flipit

**Effective Date:** October 7, 2026

Flipit ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application **Flipit** handles user information.

---

### 1. Information Collection and Use
Flipit does not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information (PII). 
- We do not require users to create an account or provide personal details such as names, phone numbers, or email addresses.
- All game progress and app settings are stored locally on your device.

*(註：若 App 有使用廣告或分析工具如 Google AdMob、Firebase，請改用下方「第 2 點」；若完全單機無網路傳輸，可寫「We do not use any third-party SDKs that track or collect user data.」)*

### 2. Data Retention and Deletion
Since we do not collect personal data on remote servers, we do not retain your personal information. You can delete all local application data at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing the app cache via your device settings.

### 3. Children’s Privacy
Flipit does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

### 4. Security
We value your trust in using Flipit. Since all data remains locally on your device, we rely on your device's operating system security features to safeguard your data.

### 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted directly on this page.
